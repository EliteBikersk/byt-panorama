Visitor statistics (Google Analytics)

  • Only with consent. Until you click “Accept”, Google Analytics is not loaded and nothing is sent.
  • What is measured: pages and parts of the page viewed and time spent in them, clicks on buttons, phone, SMS and photos, device and browser type, language, approximate location (city or country) and the referring page. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. We do not learn your name or contact details.
  • Who processes it: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, as our processor under its data processing terms. Data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • No advertising: the data is not used for ads or personalisation. Advertising storage and ad personalisation are switched off on this site.
  • How long: cookies 2 years, detailed data in Google Analytics at most 14 months.

More on how Google uses data: policies.google.com.

Map (Google Maps)

The map in the Location section does not load automatically. Only when you click “Show map” or allow it in the settings does your browser connect to Google Maps (Google Ireland Limited). Google then receives your IP address and may set its own cookies under its policy. Your choice is remembered for 12 months. Without the map you can use the “Open in Google Maps” link.

Running the site

To display the site and protect it from attacks, the web hosting provider technically processes your IP address, the time of the visit and browser details on every visit, for as long as needed for security. This is based on our legitimate interest in running the site securely under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, and you may object to it. The web hosting provider is based in the USA and certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision.

Phone calls and SMS

If you call or send an SMS, we see your number and message. We use them only to arrange a viewing and the rental and never pass them on. We keep them while we discuss the rental. If no rental follows, we delete them. If a lease is signed, the data is governed by the lease and the law.

How to change or withdraw consent

You can change or withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect processing before it. The “Cookie settings” button is also at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to withdraw consent at any time. Just call +421 905 555 835. You can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava.

Providing data is voluntary, you can view the listing without agreeing to statistics or the map. We carry out no automated decision-making or profiling with legal effects.
